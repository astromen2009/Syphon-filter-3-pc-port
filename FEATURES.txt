SYPHON FILTER 3 - PC  (version 0.9.0)
=====================================

WHAT IT IS
  Runs the original Syphon Filter 3 (USA disc, SCUS_946.40) and draws it on
  the PC. The game itself is unchanged: AI, missions, combat and animations
  are the original ones. You need your own copy of the disc (.bin + .cue).

GRAPHICS
  - Native PC renderer (PsyCross): any internal resolution (1080p, 1440p,
    4K...), MSAA 2x/4x/8x or SMAA anti-aliasing, pixelated, bilinear,
    trilinear or anisotropic texture filtering.
  - PGXP: vertices and textures no longer wobble; perspective-correct
    texturing and a depth buffer (nothing is drawn in front of what it
    should be behind).
  - True widescreen (Hor+): you see more at the sides, nothing is stretched.
    Original 4:3 available.
  - Smooth motion: the game runs on its own thread at its original speed and
    the picture is interpolated to the monitor's refresh rate. V-sync and FPS
    limit (30 to 240) configurable.
  - Distant characters keep their full model (the game used a simplified one).
  - HUD redrawn at PC resolution, sharp; adjustable size and an option to
    move it to the screen edges.
  - Live, smoothed scene lighting; broken lights really go out.
  - Muzzle flashes light the floor, walls and ceiling; gun smoke; blue taser
    light.
  - 3D effects: blood (sprays and wounds), breaking glass, sparks, bullet
    marks, tracers, fire.
  - Character shadows.
  - Snow and breath vapour in cold missions.
  - Characters stand properly on the ground (no buried or floating feet).
  - Game videos enlarged or as on the PS1.

SCOPES
  - AU300 X-ray scope REBUILT: inside the scope box the PC draws the real
    scene with the same camera as the normal view, removing the walls it
    looks through. It lines up with the outside view, has no lag when the
    scope moves, is sharp and keeps the dark PS1 look. Characters and the
    weapons they carry are seen through the wall in green. Tunable in
    sf3_nativo.ini (rayosx_*).
  - Night vision (Dragunov) like the original, also in widescreen.
  - Lock-on reticle (R1) and scope blades adapted to widescreen.

CONTROLS
  - 1:1 mouse turning and aiming (like the Syphon Filter 1 port), adjustable
    sensitivity and inversion; mouse wheel switches weapons.
  - Configurable keyboard and gamepad (key and pad window in the launcher),
    analog pad as a DualShock, rumble.
  - Classic (original) or modern movement.
  - Second player (pad or keyboard) for the two-player mode.

WEAPONS
  - Own weapon bank: any weapon shows up with its model, texture, photo in
    the Weapons menu and its sound in any mission.
  - Weapons from Syphon Filter 1 and 2: flamethrower, Gatling gun, hand
    taser and Stryker shotgun, with icons and photos.
  - Weapon sounds configurable with WAV files (armas.txt).
  (Needs armas.pak.)

LANGUAGES
  - Text and voices are chosen separately.
  - English: the original game.
  - Spanish: text, voices, video audio and menus.
  - Russian: text (Cyrillic font, menus) and full dub.
  (Spanish and Russian need idiomas.pak.)

MENUS AND CONVENIENCE
  - Windows launcher: play, settings (graphics, sound, controls, language)
    and exit.
  - PC options with F12 at any time (graphics, controls, sound, save and
    load game).
  - Cheats in F12 > CHEATS: unlock all, invulnerable, all weapons, invisible.
  - Memory card saved as files, with its own save list; "Continue" in the
    main menu.
  - High-quality main menu: the upscaled title video
    (videos_propios\TITLE_alta.mpg; CONVERTIR_MENU.bat and INTERPOLAR_MENU.bat
    help prepare it, at 30 or 60 frames per second).
  - Optional fast loading (turbo).

CHARACTERS
  - Extra characters for the mini-game and the two-player mode (personajes
    folder), with their own name and portrait; the same roster in both modes.

FILES
  bin\sf3_nativo.exe      the game
  bin\SDL2.dll            required
  bin\CREAR_PAKS.bat      joins the language and weapon files into their .pak
  bin\AJUSTES_INI.txt     every sf3_nativo.ini setting explained (Spanish)
  idiomas.pak, armas.pak  optional data (not included); next to the .exe or
                          up to three folders above
  sf3_nativo.ini          settings (created automatically)
  partidas\               saves and memory card

KEYS
  F12        PC options (and cheats)
  Esc        game pause menu / back
  Alt+Enter  full screen
